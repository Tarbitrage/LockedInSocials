# Limitations and distribution notes

These are unofficial, experimental wrappers around the third-party mobile websites. They are not replacements for the native applications.

## Functional limitations

- Instagram/YouTube can change their page markup without notice and cause false negatives or incorrect hiding.
- Some Reel/Shorts viewers can be opened through application-specific navigation that the current filters do not detect.
- Instagram DMs may lack native-only features, notifications, calls, or some media types.
- Google may refuse login in embedded WebViews; subscriptions and personalisation may be inaccessible.
- Rendering, autoplay, DRM, uploads and account flows depend on the third-party websites.
- There is no guarantee that filters work after every website or iOS update.

## Brand and service terms

Meta, Instagram, Google, YouTube and their logos are third-party trademarks. These projects are not affiliated with them. Their names, websites, and user-created content remain subject to their owners' policies and rights.

**Do not assume that publishing source code entitles you to distribute a commercial third-party client.** App Store review, Meta/Google service terms, trademark restrictions and permissions must be evaluated separately before public deployment.

## Distribution

- The source code may be built on a Mac with Xcode by someone using their own Apple Account.
- GitHub does not directly install unsigned iOS apps on other people's phones.
- TestFlight, Ad Hoc and App Store distribution have different paid membership, signing and review requirements.
- Ad Hoc device registration is limited and is not a general public software distribution route.
